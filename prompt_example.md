Here are **5 sample prompts** tailored for whiteboard explainers, ranging from tech and business to science and personal development.

### 1. Artificial Intelligence Explainer (Step-by-Step Flow)
> *"Create a whiteboard explainer showing how Artificial Intelligence works. Title at the top: 'How Artificial Intelligence Works'. Create a 3-step horizontal workflow: Step 1 'Human Prompt' with a coder/developer avatar, connected by a curved arrow to Step 2 'Neural Processing' with an AI brain illustration, connected by an arrow to Step 3 'Smart Solution' with an idea lightbulb doodle. Add clear step titles and subtitles below each."*

---

### 2. Business Startup & Growth Strategy
> *"Design a whiteboard presentation slide about Startup Growth Strategy. Top headline: 'From Idea to Market Leader'. Show a founder/presenter character on the left pitching an idea, an arrow pointing to a rising business growth chart in the middle, and a verified checkmark badge on the right representing successful market product fit. Include motivating step labels."*

---

### 3. Scientific Method / Education
> *"Make a clean educational whiteboard slide explaining The Scientific Method. Header: 'The 3 Steps of Scientific Discovery'. Display an open reading book for Step 1 'Research', a science chemistry flask for Step 2 'Experimentation', and a wise owl or lightbulb for Step 3 'Insight & Discovery', connected with flow arrows."*

---

### 4. Problem vs Solution Comparison
> *"Create a whiteboard comparison slide titled 'Traditional Workflow vs AI Automation'. On the left side, show a confused person thinking with red warning text 'Manual Bottlenecks'. In the center, put a two-way direction arrow. On the right side, show a robot assistant with green success text 'Automated Efficiency'."*

---

### 5. Habit Building & Productivity
> *"Create a whiteboard explainer titled 'The 3 Pillars of High Productivity'. Column 1: 'Clear Focus' with a bullseye target icon. Column 2: 'Continuous Learning' with a graduation cap. Column 3: 'Rapid Execution' with a startup rocket ship doodle. Use arrows between the columns and numbered bullet labels."*

---

### 💡 Pro Tip for Best Results:
When creating your own prompts, follow this simple formula:
1. **Headline**: What is the slide title?
2. **Layout Structure**: 2 columns, 3 horizontal steps, or a central illustration with callouts?
3. **Key Concepts**: Mention characters, tech, charts, or arrows (e.g., *"use arrows to link step 1 and step 2"*).
